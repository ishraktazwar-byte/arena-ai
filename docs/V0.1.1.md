# V0.1.1 — risk gates and basic local eating

## Scope
One coherent increment: deterministic survival risk gates and eating, independent
of any LLM. Alice/Bob both use the same controller; personalities remain data.

- Risk assessment distinguishes background creepers, nearby ordinary hostiles,
  close creepers, critical health, missing vitals, low oxygen and hazardous blocks.
- Arbiter admission floor cancels lower-priority work and prevents immediate
  restart while danger persists. Floor release waits 1.5 seconds after the last
  equally severe assessment to avoid rapid stop/restart oscillation.
- Safe-food allowlist, hunger/health checks, one eating session at a time,
  eight-second timeout and five-second minimum attempt interval.
- Hostiles within six blocks prevent eating, including during critical health.
- Equipment/consume calls use ownership checks; no consume follows an equipment
  await if that action was cancelled. Death/disconnect disable the controller.
- Diagnostics on changes, not every physics tick; explicit missing-food reporting.

## Important limitations
This is **not complete autonomous survival**. HALT revokes ordinary actions but
cannot escape an explosion, lava or drowning. Critical-health recovery does not
fight or flee. Do not leave this release unattended in a dangerous world.

Threat proximity is a conservative heuristic, not a reachability/line-of-sight,
fuse, projectile, armor or terrain-aware assessment. The four-block creeper
threshold is provisional, not an explosion guarantee. Neutral spiders may defer
eating. Hazard checks inspect feet/support blocks only; cliffs and future path
hazards are not covered. There is no food acquisition or automatic equipment
restoration. Third-party equip operations already in progress cannot be undone
by AbortSignal; we prevent subsequent application-level calls, and must test
library-level equipment/cancellation behavior in Minecraft separately.
