# V0.1.2 — bounded ground escape

## Scope
Terrain-validated ground retreat for close creepers and critical-health hostile
encounters. Each session considers four one-block directions, selects improved
minimum separation from nearby hostiles, and executes one bounded step. No LLM.

Full player footprint and swept segment must have known full-cube support, air
at feet/head, no hazardous/slippery/falling support, and no cliffs/ceiling blocks.
No jump, dig, swim, diagonal shortcut, unknown-chunk movement or long-range path.
Revalidate after aiming and every 50ms while walking. Reassess live threat
positions; cancel when risk disappears or the step is no longer appropriate.
Each motion is bounded to 700ms inside a 1000ms action session. Blocked routes
retry at most once per second and emit diagnostics only when the reason changes.

## Tests
29 automated tests pass under sandbox Node 22.22.3 and Node 24.21.0. Syntax checks
pass. Includes terrain/footprint fixtures, unknown terrain, competing threats,
geometry changing while aiming, cancellation during aim, bounded no-progress
failure, simulated successful walking, and survival-controller integration.

## Limitations
Not live tested. No guaranteed creeper survival, fuse prediction, knockback
compensation, swimming, lava escape, vertical navigation or general pathfinding.
Ground validation deliberately rejects many traversable shapes such as stairs.
Server physics, latency, and already-issued equipment/look operations need live
validation. No action claims a real-world result based solely on simulated tests.
