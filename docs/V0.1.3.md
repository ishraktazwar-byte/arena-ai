# V0.1.3 — stable local melee

## Scope
One shared combat controller for all agent profiles. Healthy, fed agents with
usable weapons engage supported nearby ordinary hostiles. Close skeletons may
be fought with melee; distant skeletons do not force ranged-only behavior.
Current relevant targets are retained over slightly closer ordinary enemies.
Immediate creepers and critical health override combat through local survival.

- Heuristic weapon selection supports swords, axes and emergency mining tools.
- Equipment-specific conservative attack cooldowns; no every-tick attack spam.
- Ground-only approach uses V0.1.2 swept-footprint validation.
- Re-observe after approach in a new session; revalidate target, weapon, reach
  and conservative clear line immediately before each attack command.
- Attack diagnostics distinguish a command from a confirmed hit.
- Three failures suppress an unreachable target for five seconds, then reassess.
- Action priority 500, below eating (700) and emergency escape (1000).

## Validation
45 automated tests pass on Node 22.22.3 and Node 24.21.0; syntax checks pass.
Includes spider/zombie/husk/close-skeleton/stray/pillager policy, target stability,
near vs distant creeper arbitration, cooldowns, unknown geometry/walls, death
while equipping, target disappearance while aiming, immediate threat preemption,
and bounded unreachable-target retries. Prior foundation/survival/escape tests
remain passing. No Windows or live Minecraft validation is claimed.

## Limitations
No ranged shots or tactical shields yet. Weapon score is a heuristic, not an
accurate model of enchantments, armor, durability, potion effects or Minecraft
DPS. No strafing, sprint attacks, jumping, group tactics or general pathfinding.
Health 7–11 deliberately avoids initiating combat; full tactical withdrawal at
those intermediate health levels is not yet implemented. Species and proximity
alone do not prove hostility (e.g. daytime spiders). No player attacks.
