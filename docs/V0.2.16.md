# V0.2.16 — connected farm production and observed growth recovery

## Capability status

The four requested components are implemented together through FarmManager:
**tilling, irrigation construction, guarded food processing, and growth
monitoring/recovery**. The existing prepared-plot goal is not being relabeled as
full production. This release adds an agent-selected `establish_farm` intention.

The combined local-production capability is implemented and automated-tested,
**awaiting live validation**. The broader civilization platform is incomplete.
Growth is probabilistic and server-dependent; no guaranteed harvest time, endless
food, rollback or exclusive ownership/causality is claimed.

## Goal, location and supplies

`establish_farm {x,y,z,crop,targetStock,reserve}` takes the same six typed fields as
`manage_farm`: a nearby crop-height center, wheat/carrots/potatoes/beetroots and
integer counts 1–64. Internally it persists `develop:true`. The model chooses
whether to farm, the destination, crop and target; bounded read-only ordinary-soil
site proposals ground that choice. The worker does not follow a fixed life story.

`manage_farm` remains prepared-plot maintenance. `stop_farm` abandons the intention;
console `stop` only cancels current work, which a durable intention may resume.
`quit` stops the runtime. Enabled strategic AI, persistent memory and the existing
four farming/workspace/navigation/collection scopes enable development. Dedicated
`autonomous_world` policy needs no per-area/action approval. Restricted mode remains
an optional shared-server policy; it is not ownership detection.

The bounded 5×5 layout, relative to the crop-height center, reserves:

| Cell | Offset x,y,z |
| --- | --- |
| Contained water pocket | -2,-1,-2 |
| Crafting table | -2,0,+2 |
| Furnace | +2,0,-2 |
| Cobblestone lamp support | 0,0,0 |
| Torch | 0,+1,0 |

The pocket's cardinal walls are not tilled. Infrastructure is not planted over.
Empty suitable dirt/grass/coarse-dirt/path cells can be cultivated; existing
obstructions are diagnosed, not bulldozed. There is no arbitrary terrain grading,
house building or automatic storage. Source search is bounded to four horizontal
blocks around the body and the same/below layer, not a global water finder.

Tools, planting stock, coal/charcoal, lamp/workstation materials and a bucket must
be carried or obtainable through the finite supported crafting operations.
Raw iron can be smelted for a bucket; this is not automatic long-distance ore
exploration/mining. Source extraction requires a level-zero water block with two
cardinal source neighbors and suitable support. A carried water bucket is an
alternative. Nether water placement is refused. Shortages remain visible to
strategy instead of being treated as completed construction.

## Physical safeguards and evidence

- Tilling requires a usable hoe, clear soil, safe footing/body separation and
  current permission. Coarse dirt takes two separate verified operations. Success
  needs a raw server transformation and confirmed retained tool count.
- Excavation requires a usable shovel, inert full bottom/four walls, empty space
  above and no supporting-body removal. Fill requires an empty contained pocket.
  Bucket rays traverse exact voxel intervals so a narrow rim intersection cannot
  be skipped by fixed-distance samples. Water use sends pinned 1.21.1 rotation.
- Water pickup/placement requires both raw world evidence and authoritative
  bucket ↔ water-bucket inventory conversion. Natural source renewal after the
  extraction report is supported. Water coverage is re-observed, not inferred
  merely from an animation or one successful click.
- Finite block placement requires a safe supported empty cell, retained exit,
  permission, server block report and item decrease. Interactive support blocks
  are refused. Farmland conversion beneath a placed solid block also requires
  soil permission. Item drops are non-colliding, not treated as living occupants;
  construction/water can affect loose items without claiming ownership.
- Aim, inventory staging and every continuation are body/session guarded. Current
  conditions are checked after confirmation too. Cancellation does not roll back
  a packet already accepted by the server; the next decision re-observes.

## Food and furnace operations

Potatoes become baked potatoes. Wheat becomes bread through guarded crafting.
Carrots/beetroot remain usable raw food. The furnace primitive also supports beef,
porkchop, chicken, mutton, rabbit, cod and salmon; a separate typed tool smelts raw
iron. No hunting or arbitrary furnace recipes are included.

Each furnace action performs one fresh phase: load one input, supply one allowed
fuel if needed, observe processing, or recover the complete bounded output stack
into an empty main-inventory slot. Loading/fuel are explicitly **not** cooked-food
claims. Raw window snapshots verify transfers and recovered output. Foreign
inputs/fuel/output, malformed inventory and unavailable output space fail closed.
Hotbar ingredients/fuel use guarded number-key staging, not unbounded transfer or
shift-click helpers. Fuel burning immediately after insertion is handled.

Furnace/table opens share the late-response quarantine: an unresolved opening
cannot later bind to a new action; reconnect is required. A later fresh invocation
can recover output with no remaining raw ingredient. The worker tracks pending
food/iron processing and periodically probes after restart, not replaying clicks.

Production reserves include the configured seed buffer **plus known bare beds**,
so cooking cannot starve replanting or create a perpetual harvest/cook deficit.
Limited initial planting stock can expand through a verified bootstrap harvest
and pickup. Missing bootstrap yield blocks further depletion; that fact persists
across restart. Food below 12 can override edible reserves. Hungry stationary
food processing is allowed when otherwise safe; movement retains its hunger gate.

## Growth: measured conditions, not guarantees

The worker observes actual crop age, soil moisture, nearby hydration coverage and
above-crop block/sky light. Flowing water may hydrate; only suitable source water
may be extracted. Unknown terrain/light is not called healthy. Skylight alone is
variable with time/weather; installed lighting still needs observed block light.

At most 25 nearby cells are tracked. Unchanged age for 20 minutes of continuous
nearby observation is a **suspected** stall. Gaps over 60 seconds, age/scope changes
and time regression reset elapsed observation; offline/unloaded time is not counted
as failed growth. The worker repairs supported missing water/lamp infrastructure.
For an immature, wet, lit suspected stall it may apply carried bone meal, throttled
per cell to ten minutes. That action needs raw age increase and item use; failure
is not called recovery. Unsupported terrain, unavailable materials, blocked access,
server random-tick settings or permissions can leave a diagnosed unresolved stall.

## Persistence and automated verification

Schema **13** reads schemas 1–12 and upgrades on write. Older envelopes cannot
smuggle development permission. World/dimension-scoped intentions and bounded
bootstrap-progress facts persist; executable queues and elapsed offline growth do
not. Older binaries refuse the newer schema; preserve memory backups before any
version downgrade.

**496 tests** include the previous 453 regressions and 43 new cases. A connected
fixture drives the actual manager/tool registry and guarded tools from ordinary
dirt and two potatoes through water acquisition, construction, tilling, limited-
stock expansion, planting, harvest, verified pickup and cooked food. The fixture
starts with tools/workstation items/fuel and an empty bucket; it simulates movement,
server replies, lighting, hydration, random crop-age changes and furnace ticks.
It is not a from-nothing live-world experiment. Separate pinned recipe tests cover
bucket, furnace, torch and bread recipes.

Additional tests exercise cancellation/late replies, unsupported/unknown terrain,
permissions/body changes, finite/flowing source refusal, exact thin-rim geometry,
raw evidence rejection, immediate fuel consumption, hotbar supplies, foreign
furnace contents, full inventory, output recovery, seed budgets, disk restart,
growth diagnosis/throttling and grounded site proposals. The 1.21.1 protocol
serializer accepts the produced bucket packet.
