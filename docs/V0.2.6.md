# V0.2.6 — needs-aware planning and failed-action retry protection

## Implementation-first milestone

Live testing remains deferred at the user's direction. This release improves
strategic context and repetition control, not the claimed readiness of the whole
civilization platform. No live Minecraft connections or cloud-provider requests
are needed for or claimed by this release's automated validation.

## Advisory needs, not scripted progression

Runtime observations now include deterministic local risk, whether inventory is
known, empty normal-inventory slots, and six bounded needs:

| Need | Evidence | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Safety | Local risk, known vitals and position | Local reflex |
| Recovery | Health deficit | Local reflex |
| Nutrition | Food-level deficit | Local reflex |
| Food reserve | Carried units from the existing safe-food policy; initial target 4 units | Strategy |
| Inventory space | Empty normal slots, not number of item types | Strategy |
| Gathering tool | Whether a recognized pickaxe is carried | Strategy |

Each need has a stable ID, score from 0–100 (or null for unknown evidence), status,
owner and structured evidence. Supply thresholds are initial deterministic policy,
not learned preferences. Food units are not calories or a promised meal duration.
Pickaxe presence does not assert durability, appropriate tier or mining eligibility.
Unknown inventory does not mean zero supplies. Missing vitals, position or unknown
risk keep the safety need high. No arbitrary item NBT or display text enters needs.

The planner may address feasible supply needs using its actual runtime catalog.
It receives no house → farm → king script, and no new authority. A full-inventory
need does not grant permission to toss items or open containers. An absent food
reserve does not invent farming, hunting, cooking or food-acquisition skills.
Local safety floors, combat/eating controllers and guarded tools remain unchanged.
Needs are recomputed observations, not durable memory records; schema 5 stays
unchanged. Existing data-driven agent personalities remain planning context.

## Bounded retry ledger

FAILED and BLOCKED execution outcomes record the validated tool and exact arguments,
dimension and origin block. Argument order, different reason text and UUID casing
cannot evade an otherwise identical key. First failure delays the same action for
10 minutes; another failed execution raises that to 20, then at most 30 minutes.
Clock lookups do not extend cooldowns. A successful completion clears that key;
CANCELLED outcomes do not create or extend suppression. Stale, stopped or reflex-
invalidated executions remain historical outcomes but do not alter the ledger.
Thrown execution exceptions become sanitized FAILED outcomes under the same policy.

A provider receives at most eight active same-dimension records, including origin
cell and remaining delay. If it nevertheless proposes a cooling-down action at
that origin, the controller emits STRATEGY-DEFER and substitutes a validated
read-only scan. It does not call the provider again, remove permission checks or
silently choose a different mutation. Read-only observations are never suppressed.
The existing request budget, scheduling interval and stale-plan checks remain.

This is conservative repetition control, not a security boundary or a learned
world map. Changed origins/arguments are different attempts; an inventory/terrain
change at the same origin does not automatically clear an existing cooldown.
No claim is made that a failed or cancelled action had no partial effects.
At most 64 records are retained for one hour after their last recorded failure;
backwards clock movement discards affected entries. Records are owned by one
strategy controller, survive its stop/start, and reset on process restart. They
are not shared across bots or persisted to memory. The controller remains scoped
to its current connection/world; no world-switch or reconnect feature is added.

## Automated checks

New deterministic tests cover needs ordering, unknown data, local ownership,
safe-food exclusions, carried-tool uncertainty, inventory capacity and runtime
integration. Fake-clock retry tests cover exact identities, UUID normalization,
expiration/backoff bounds, successful clearing, cancellation, read-only immunity,
bounded retention, copy isolation, dimension separation and backwards clocks.
Controller tests cover actual scan substitution, provider context, no additional
provider requests, different allowed actions, disabled collection permission,
stale/reflex-invalidated results and exception sanitization.

All previous resource, collection, crafting, workspace, survival, combat, provider,
memory and cancellation tests remain part of the full regression suite.

## Still unfinished

Ranged tactics, richer resource memory, complete gathering/navigation, broader
crafting and food production, relationships, settlements, economies, diplomacy
and multi-civilization systems remain future work. This version is a tested
increment toward a mature implementation, not the final live-validation milestone.
